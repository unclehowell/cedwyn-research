MR-0005 Not Found

Conveyance on sale of 25 December 1938 (Mountjoy / Bute to Western Ground Rents Ltd), with plan and land schedules

Should exist. Not held.

Why: Cross-reference (EV-0216); conveyancing practice (deeds pass to the purchaser).

Learn more

Later WGR deeds deduce title from "a Conveyance on sale dated 25th December 1938" (EV-0216). As a root of title it passed with the deeds to each buyer, so it should be in the BP deeds packet received in 1969 and among the documents lodged at first registration in 1982–83.

Who should hold it: BP Properties Ltd / successors (deeds packet); HM Land Registry first-registration files WA231076 and WA240304; Companies House / The National Archives (WGR file BT 31/87651)

Date of the record: 1938-12-25
