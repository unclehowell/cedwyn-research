Bute Estate Records R 1/43–59: Country rentals, 1876–1893 (National Library of Wales)

Located in the National Library of Wales catalogue; the volumes are not yet seen.

Why: Cross-reference (National Library of Wales catalogue: file R 1/43–59 'Country rentals, 1876–1893', MMS 992088095302419, within series R 1 'Glamorgan Estate rentals, 1728–1893', MMS 992088177902419, catalogue entry EV-0698; read through the library's public discovery interface on 8 Nov 2026).

In 1876/77 the Bute estate districts were reorganised and the remaining Glamorgan Estate rental was renamed the Country rental; it runs to 1892/93, and the districts it lists include Leckwith and Llandough. The file R 1/43–59 holds seventeen annual volumes, from R 1/43 (1876–7) to R 1/59 (1892–3). They would show who paid rent to the Bute estate for holdings in Llandough and Leckwith in each of those years, including 1876–78, and at what rent. Against: a rental lists tenants, holdings and sums, and does not identify the Tŷ Mawr parcel unless an entry names it; which Llandough entries these volumes carry is not checked.

Who should hold it: National Library of Wales, Aberystwyth: Bute Estate Records (GB 0210 BUTE), R 1/43–59

Date of the record: 1876–1893
