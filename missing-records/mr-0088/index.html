MR-0088 Not Held By The Wildlife Trust; Possible Copies At Sewbrec, Not Yet Asked

Glamorgan County Naturalists' Trust site files, survey records, maps and photographs for Llandough and Leckwith, 1961–2002

The Wildlife Trust holds nothing on the site now; it wrote that some of the relevant paperwork was lost in a fire in about 2003.

The Glamorgan County Naturalists' Trust, founded in January 1961 and later the Glamorgan Wildlife Trust, merged into the present Wildlife Trust of South and West Wales in 2002. A county naturalists' trust of that period kept site files and survey notes on woodland and grassland it watched or proposed for protection, and the wooded slopes between Llandough and Leckwith were notified as an SSSI (0524) in the same years (MR-0083). Such files would record who was approached as owner or occupier of the land, a third party's contemporary account of who held and worked it. On 7 Nov 2026 the Trust replied that it does not hold any data on the site, that it 'may have had some historically', and that some of the paperwork was lost in a fire in about 2003; it pointed to the digitised archive of the naturalist Mary Gillham, held by the South East Wales Biodiversity Records Centre (SEWBReC), as the most likely home of anything that survived. Against: the Trust could not say any file on this site ever existed; a naturalist's survey report would describe habitat and species, not tenure, and may not name the farm. Parcel not identified: the woodland (Parcel A woodland) and the Leckwith slopes are the likely subjects; a grid reference or site name in a surviving report would identify it.

Who should hold it: South East Wales Biodiversity Records Centre (digitised Mary Gillham archive), not yet asked. The Wildlife Trust of South and West Wales holds nothing in the office (reply of 7 Nov 2026).

Date of the record: 1961-2002
