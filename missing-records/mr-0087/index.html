MR-0087 Not Yet Asked

The applicant's copy of the list of documents delivered with the February 1983 first-registration application for the farmhouse and garden (title WA240304)

The 1925 rules required a list of the documents lodged in triplicate; the Registry's copy is MR-0020, the applicant's copy has not been looked for.

Why: Statute (Land Registration Rules 1925, r.20(i)-(iv), text read in a c.1930 printing of the rules); cross-reference (HM Land Registry document list for WA240304, 30 Jul 2026; MR-0020); practice (return of deeds with the applicant's copy, not checked).

Learn more

Rule 20 of the Land Registration Rules 1925, as printed in a statute collection of about 1930, required an application for first registration to be accompanied by every original deed and document relating to the title in the applicant's possession or control (abstracts of title, contracts and counsel's opinions included), a copy or abstract of the latest document of title, particulars by plan, and 'a list in triplicate of all documents delivered'. The application for the farmhouse and garden (WA240304) was made by BP Properties Ltd on 23 February 1983. A list in three copies should therefore have existed, and in ordinary conveyancing practice one copy comes back to the applicant's solicitors with the returned deeds (practice, not checked for this file). That list would show which documents BP Properties Ltd showed as its title: the May 1975 conveyance (MR-0050), and whether the 1938 conveyance, the 1969 conveyance or any abstract went in. The Registry's own copy and examination papers are MR-0020; the Registry's document list of 30 July 2026 keeps only the 1975 conveyance and plans for this title. Against: the rule allowed the Registrar to direct otherwise; amendments to rule 20 between 1930 and 1983 are not checked; a list that does not survive is a records gap, not evidence of anything done wrong.

Who should hold it: The applicant's side: the deed records of BP Properties Ltd and its successors, or the solicitors who lodged the 1983 application. The Registry's copy is MR-0020.

Date of the record: 1983-02
